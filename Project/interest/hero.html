Interests Hero

Yusuke Nogi

Yusuke Nogi had a difficult childhood. He experienced childhood trauma and was bullied when he was young. Because of these experiences, he worked hard to become stronger and studied seriously. He later graduated from a military academy and became a highly skilled intelligence officer. Even though he is very capable, he does not show off his abilities and usually appears to be an ordinary person. He deeply loves his country and cares about the people around him.

Actor:
Yusuke Nogi is played by Masato Sakai.
Multilingual:
He is multilingual and can communicate in around ten languages, including Japanese, English, and Mongolian.
Skill:
Nogi is highly skilled in firearms, marksmanship, hand-to-hand combat, explosives, cybersecurity, map reading, surveillance, eavesdropping, intelligence gathering, and strategic planning.
